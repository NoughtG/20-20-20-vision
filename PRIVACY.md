# Privacy Policy for Farsight

**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

Farsight ("we", "our", or "the app") was built with privacy as a foundational principle. This Privacy Policy explains how Farsight handles your information when you use our macOS application.

---

### 1. The Core Principle: 100% Local, Zero Data Collection

Farsight is designed to run entirely on your Mac. We do not operate external tracking servers, do not require user accounts, and **never collect, sell, store, or transmit any personal information**.

---

### 2. Camera Access & Presence Detection

Farsight includes an **optional, opt-in feature** called Presence Detection:

- **Strictly Opt-In:** The app will never request camera access until you explicitly toggle the feature on in Settings.
- **On-Device Local Processing Only:** When enabled, Farsight uses Apple’s native `AVFoundation` and `Vision` frameworks to inspect a single, low-resolution frame once every 60 seconds solely to verify if a face is present.
- **No Recording or Storage:** Camera frames are analyzed in temporary memory and **immediately discarded**. Farsight never saves photos, never records video, and never transmits any visual data over the internet.
- **Hardware Indicator:** Your Mac’s green camera indicator light will only activate for a fraction of a second during each scheduled snapshot check.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage

All data generated by Farsight remains on your local Mac:

- **Break Settings:** Your preferred intervals, break durations, and notification toggles are stored in `UserDefaults`.
- **Break History:** Completed break timestamps and streak metrics are stored in a local JSON file on your machine (`~/Library/Application Support/Farsight/break_events.json`).

This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you remove the application.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Farsight does **not** integrate any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or external telemetry libraries.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

Farsight does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, the updated version will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
