Privacy Policy for Farsight

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. 100% Local, Zero Data Collection

Farsight is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. The app runs entirely on your Mac. We do not operate external servers, do not require user accounts, and never collect, sell, store, or transmit any personal data.

2. Camera Access & Presence Detection

Farsight includes an optional, opt-in camera presence check:

  • Strictly Opt-In: The app will never request camera access until you explicitly toggle the feature on in Settings.
  • On-Device Analysis Only: When enabled, Farsight uses Apple’s native Vision framework to inspect a single frame every 60 seconds solely to determine if a face is present.
  • Zero Retention: Frames are processed in volatile memory and immediately discarded. Nothing is ever saved to disk or transmitted over the network.

3. Local Storage

Your preferences and streak history are stored strictly in local files on your Mac (UserDefaults and local Application Support). They never leave your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Farsight does not include any analytics SDKs, tracking libraries, or third-party advertising networks.

5. Contact Us

For questions or support, visit our GitHub repository: github.com/NoughtG/20-20-20-vision.
